Intentional Fatherhood Podcast – Episode 6: Marriage, Digital Hygiene, Discipline, Rhythms, and Dating Your Spouse

Core Ideas

  • Family life already runs on habits, and children absorb them: "we become our habits and our children become us." (21:05-21:08)
  • God forms families in ordinary, messy moments, not only in deep talks or devotions. (14:42-14:51)
  • Rhythms can change your knee-jerk reaction to ordinary chaos. (14:12-14:17)
  • Love means willing another person's good, and romantic feeling tends to follow that action. (26:33-26:39)
  • You practiced your way into hard patterns, so you practice your way out. (49:09-49:16)

Actionable Takeaways

  • Write a short bedtime blessing and say it nightly. It will feel awkward at first, so adapt the words to each child's age. (10:19-10:25)
  • Before disciplining, pause and pray briefly, even just "Lord, help." (52:37-52:45)
  • When a child melts down, get curious before correcting. Ask what's behind it: sleep, hunger, overstimulation. (59:34-59:38)
  • Set a weekly date night as a rhythm, not a rule, even when you're tired. (45:32-45:43)
  • Study your wife and kids to learn how each one feels most loved, then act on it. (29:18-29:21)
  • Set household device rules, such as keeping devices out of bathrooms and closed rooms. (71:29-71:35)
  • Model what you ask of your kids: "Do not ask your kids to do something that you... are not doing." (63:20-63:24)

Notable Quotes

  • "Nothing is normal in the household until it is." (12:32-12:37)
  • "It's worth the fight." (41:13-41:19)
  • "These rhythms won't change God's love for you, but God's love for you should change your rhythms." (73:19-73:24)
