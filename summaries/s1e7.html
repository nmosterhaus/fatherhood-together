Intentional Fatherhood Podcast – Episode 7: Made to Work, Sanctification Through Your Vocation + Staying Busy But Balanced

Core Ideas

  • God made you to work and to shape the world, a calling rooted in Genesis (13:52-14:04).
  • Meaningful work matters: without it you don't thrive, and unemployment or underemployment hurts more than overemployment (15:19-15:35).
  • Work both hurts and sanctifies. The long discovery process keeps you dependent on Jesus (30:46-31:09, 33:40-33:44).
  • Being too busy and being too idle both damage family, so aim for "busy, but balanced" (35:31-35:44).
  • Work must not become an escape from family (38:59-39:14).

Actionable Takeaways

  • Use the three-circle test to find vocation: what you can do, what you love, and what the world needs (22:02-22:52).
  • Set a travel limit (the speaker uses about two trips a month, two nights each) and treat anything beyond it as a decision that must be worth it (36:56-37:04).
  • Check whether overwork is hiding a deeper issue, and be honest about it (38:34-38:43).
  • Build a daily rhythm: phone off in the evening, family dinner, morning prayer, bedtime liturgy (39:49-40:32).
  • Before a career move, ask your spouse and wise mentors to speak into the decision (28:08-28:20).
  • If your job damages your family, it's okay to say no and pray about other opportunities (42:02-42:19).

Notable Quotes

  • "Work makes pain, but work sanctifies." (33:40-33:44)
  • "Your fatherhood needs you to find work." (30:37-30:38)
  • "Busy, but balanced." (35:31-35:36)
  • "Unemployment is way worse than overemployment." (15:19-15:25)
