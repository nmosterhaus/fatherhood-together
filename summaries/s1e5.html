[ fatherhood + together ]

Intentional Fatherhood Podcast – Episode 5: Your Fatherhood Needs You to Know Your Story, Facing Generational Sin, Trusting God to Bless What’s Been Broken, and Practical Steps Toward Health

Core Ideas

  • Your past shapes your fatherhood now, often before you were born; generational patterns are real but not destiny (1:54-2:14 is not it, see 1:02:42-1:03:00 and 1:08:11-1:08:20 context; key framing at 4:59-5:08)
  • Unexamined pain (the "locusts") drives reactions: being "triggered" is time travel back to an old wound, and it shows up in work, marriage, and parenting (34:37-35:20)
  • Dysfunctional family systems assign roles (overly responsible, clown, scapegoat) that carry into adulthood (25:43-26:44)
  • God redeems brokenness and builds on blessings, sometimes through people he sends, like a spiritual father (58:38-59:30)
  • Breaking the "you'll become your father" lie matters; you can choose a different path (53:55-54:15)

Actionable Takeaways

  • Pray first and ask God for a soft heart toward your story (45:22-45:46)
  • Ask your spouse, "Gently with your words, but very honest: what is it like living with me?" (47:59-48:58)
  • Find a Jesus-loving therapist and commit to the process, even when it costs time and money (46:45-47:08)
  • Expect deep therapy sessions to leave you drained; rest is part of the work (46:58-47:18)
  • Separate your pain from current circumstances; ask whether a "job problem" is really a story problem (38:35-39:17)
  • Tell your father's and family's story to a trusted friend, pastor, or group (1:08:17-1:08:37)
  • Write a letter to your father, even if you can't send it or he can't receive it (1:10:18-1:10:52)

Notable Quotes

  • "Being triggered is actually more like time travel." (34:37-34:48)
  • "Know your story so that you can grow from your story." (53:02-53:08)
  • "We don't have to be our past." (1:10:02-1:10:04)

Romans 8:28

ESV: And we know that for those who love God all things work together for good, for those who are called according to his purpose.

MSG: Meanwhile, the moment we get tired in the waiting, God’s Spirit is right alongside helping us along. If we don’t know how or what to pray, it doesn’t matter. He does our praying in and for us, making prayer out of our wordless sighs, our aching groans. He knows us far better than we know ourselves, knows our pregnant condition, and keeps us present before God. That’s why we can be so sure that every detail in our lives of love for God is worked into something good.
