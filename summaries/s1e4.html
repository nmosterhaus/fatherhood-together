Intentional Fatherhood Podcast – Episode 4: Your Fatherhood Needs Your Exercise, Eating, and Physicality to Shape a Life of Love

Core Ideas

  • Body and spirit are one unified whole. Faith that treats you as a "Gnostic brain on a stick" ignores the body and mental health (12:12-12:21).
  • Physical habits like rest, exercise, and eating are spiritual disciplines aimed at loving others, not at appearance (25:53-25:56, 46:14-46:16).
  • Body practice is necessary but not sufficient for a healthy mind. You can "garden the mind" rather than feel stuck with it (18:41-18:43, 25:23-25:26).
  • Rest comes before work. Needing sleep is a feature of being human, not a flaw (30:07-30:15).

Actionable Takeaways

  • Pick one habit, do it with one friend, and commit for one month. Start small (1:01:00-1:01:17).
  • Keep a consistent bed and wake time, and stop looking at your phone before bed (29:42-29:48).
  • Protect about seven hours of sleep a night (30:38-30:40).
  • If exercise doesn't fit your day, schedule it in a fixed window. One dad stole an hour from work rather than from family time (44:16-44:18).
  • Walk outside. A two-mile walk every third day counts (44:54-45:02).
  • Break indulge-then-shame cycles by fasting to cultivate dependence and feasting to cultivate delight, with ordinary, fair eating in between (53:04-53:07).
  • If you have an eating disorder, depression, or trauma, seek professional help. These rhythms don't replace it (55:59-56:08).

Notable Quotes

  • "I was tired of being tired. I was sick of being sick." (43:19-43:22)
