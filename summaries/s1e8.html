Intentional Fatherhood Podcast – Episode 8: Finding Meaning, Art + Beauty in Fatherhood, You Were Created to Delight + Pursue Hobbies

Core Ideas

  • Fatherhood needs you to find meaning: hobbies, art, and joy are part of being human and reflect God's own delight in creation (0:00-0:00 framing; 4:35-5:35 via "Your fatherhood needs you to find meaning" at 3:04-3:09 and 4:35-5:35)
  • Caring for your wife's needs first matters: a hobby without her space creates guilt and marital strain (16:30-17:32)
  • Hobbies can be shared with kids, so your kids see the real you and learn from your joy (30:47-31:23)
  • Work and meaning overlap for some, but even non-productive hobbies such as music, camping, or tinkering renew you (10:55-12:21)
  • Life itself is a work of art, and small daily habits are the strokes that build it (48:30-49:16)

Actionable Takeaways

  • Ask your wife what brings her life, then protect that time, such as a weekly evening or a standing morning class (16:30-17:32)
  • Tell your wife you're carrying bedtime or dishes so she can read or rest, without matching her exhaustion with your own (22:35-23:20)
  • Name one hobby that brings you life and schedule it, even if it feels unproductive (10:55-11:20)
  • Invite a child into a hobby, such as tinkering, camping, or a shared drink or cigar, so they see you happy (30:47-31:23, 35:01-35:26)
  • Start with rest before inviting kids, so you're present when you do (34:14-34:33)
  • Treat a day of rest as worshipful fun, not just absence of work (43:32-44:12)
  • Reframe duties as gifts: "I get to tinker," not "I should" (42:42-43:08)

Notable Quotes

  • "The man who works with his mind should Sabbath with his hands." (33:56-34:21)
  • "Your fatherhood needs you to find meaning." (3:04-3:09)
  • "Your family is the greatest project that I'll ever work on." (48:30-49:16)
