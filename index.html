A newly forming group for dads of children birth through elementary school age, meeting Thursday evenings at Grace Community Church in North Liberty. You do not need to attend or be a member of Grace to join us.

What This Is

This is the place to bring everything that comes with being a father — the messy, the meaningful, and everything in between — and to share, encourage, grow alongside, and be supported by others who want to live as the fully formed fathers God created us to be.

Each week we'll use an episode from the Intentional Fatherhood podcast as a jumping-off point for honest conversation about how fatherhood intersects with every other part of our lives. Together we'll find practical ways to be more intentional as fathers, husbands, and followers of Jesus — especially in a season where it can feel like there's only enough time to survive, not thrive.
